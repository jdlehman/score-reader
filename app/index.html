Score Reader

Turn a PDF or photos of sheet music into a MusicXML file, which Sectional and notation programs (MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico) can open. It all happens in your browser: your music isn’t uploaded anywhere.

Drop a PDF or photos of the pages here

Each language downloads about 6–14 MB the first time you use it, and is then kept by the browser. The music itself is the same in every language.

  • A clean PDF works best: notation software’s own PDFs, or those from CPDL or IMSLP.
  • For a paper copy, use your phone’s document scanner rather than the camera: Notes on an iPhone (tap the camera button, then Scan Documents), or Google Drive on Android (+, then Scan). It flattens the page and evens out the light, and saves a PDF.
  • Photos are checked first: if a page looks too tilted or blurry to read, you’ll be warned before the minute-long read starts, so you can rescan instead.
  • Photos of several pages: choose them all at once, in page order.

Use a computer if you can: reading takes a minute or two for the first page and up to a minute for each page after, and needs a lot of memory. The first time, the page downloads about 60 MB.
